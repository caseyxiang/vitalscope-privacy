Privacy Policy — VitalScope

Last updated: September 28, 2026

VitalScope ("we", "us", "the app") is a personal and family health management app developed by SIXPINES PRODUCTS INC. ("the Developer"). This policy explains what information VitalScope handles and how.

1. Data You Provide

VitalScope works primarily with information you choose to enter or import:

  • Medications: drug names, dosages, schedules, taken/skipped/missed history
  • Vital Signs: blood pressure, heart rate, weight, blood glucose, temperature, SpO2
  • Lab / Imaging / Medical Records: photographs of paper documents you scan in
  • Appointments: clinic name, date, time, notes
  • Family Members: nicknames and avatars you assign to people whose health you track
  • Sign in with Apple: a privacy-preserving user identifier (and optionally name / email if you grant them) used only to authenticate AI services

2. Where Your Data Lives

By default, all data stays on your device and your own personal iCloud:

  • Local: stored in SwiftData (Apple's on-device database)
  • Cloud: synced via Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud database (not the developer's servers)

The Developer cannot access your iCloud data. Apple manages encryption and access.

3. AI Recognition (Optional, On-Demand)

When you tap "智能识别 / Scan with AI" on a lab report, imaging report, medical record, prescription or drug box, the app uploads the 1–5 photos you just selected to temporary storage operated by the Developer (Supabase, United States) and asks a backend function to parse them. The function sends the images to a third-party AI provider (currently Google Gemini via the paid API, with Alibaba Cloud Qwen, Singapore region, as fallback) and returns the parsed text (lab values, drug names, dates, etc.) to your device.

  • The photos are deleted from the Developer's storage as soon as the request finishes, whether recognition succeeds or fails.
  • The AI providers process the images under their paid API terms, which do not use your content to train their models.
  • The Developer keeps a minimal usage log per request (time, provider used, token counts, success/failure) to enforce the monthly free quota. It contains no images and no health values.

You can use the entire app without ever using AI recognition. AI is opt-in per scan and clearly marked. If the service cannot be reached, you can enter the same information manually.

4. HealthKit Integration (Read-Only)

If you grant permission, VitalScope reads select data from Apple Health (vital signs and per-object medications) to populate your records. VitalScope does not write anything back to Apple Health. You can revoke access any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → VitalScope.

5. Notifications

VitalScope schedules local notifications for medication reminders. These are entirely on-device — the Developer never sees what your reminders say or when they fire.

6. Tracking & Advertising

VitalScope does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app identifiers. The privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking: false.

7. Children's Privacy

VitalScope is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a parent uses VitalScope to track a child's medication, the same on-device + private-iCloud model applies — that data does not reach the Developer's servers (except transiently for AI recognition the parent explicitly triggers).

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → 账号 (Account) → 删除账号 (Delete Account). This revokes Sign in with Apple, deletes your account and usage logs from the Developer's server, and erases all app data on your device and in your iCloud. Backups you exported manually are not touched.
  • Delete any record or family member at any time (deletes locally + from iCloud)
  • Export your data as a backup file (Settings → 备份与恢复 / Backup)
  • Disable iCloud sync in iOS Settings → [your Apple ID] → iCloud → VitalScope
  • Disable AI recognition by simply not using the AI buttons
  • Revoke HealthKit access in iOS Settings
  • Delete the app to remove all locally-stored data

9. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date at the top and push a notice in the app's "What's New" on the next App Store release.

10. Contact

Questions, complaints, or data requests:

VitalScope 隐私政策

最近更新:2026-09-28

VitalScope(下称「本应用」)由 SIXPINES PRODUCTS INC.(下称「开发者」)开发, 是一款个人与家庭健康管理工具。本政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

1. 您提供的信息

本应用主要处理您自己录入或导入的信息:

  • 用药计划:药品名、剂量、用法、服用 / 跳过 / 漏服记录
  • 体征数据:血压、心率、体重、血糖、体温、血氧
  • 化验 / 影像 / 病历:您扫描的纸质报告照片
  • 预约:医院、日期、时间、备注
  • 家庭成员:您为追踪对象设置的昵称和头像
  • Sign in with Apple:Apple 隐私保护的用户 ID(可选 name / email)用于 向 AI 服务做身份认证

2. 数据存放位置

默认情况下,所有数据都在您的设备和您本人的 iCloud 里:

  • 本地:SwiftData(苹果设备端数据库)
  • 云端:通过苹果 CloudKit 同步到您自己的 iCloud 私有库(不是开发者的服务器)

开发者无法访问您的 iCloud 数据。加密和访问控制由 Apple 管理。

3. AI 识别(可选,按需触发)

您主动点击「智能识别」时,本应用会把您刚选的 1–5 张图片上传到开发者运营的 临时存储(Supabase,美国),由后端函数转发给第三方 AI 服务(当前为 Google Gemini 付费 API,阿里云通义千问新加坡区作为备用),再把解析出的文字(化验值、药名、 日期等)返回到您的设备。

  • 请求结束后(无论识别成功或失败),图片会立即从开发者的存储中删除。
  • AI 服务商按其付费 API 条款处理图片,不会用您的内容训练模型。
  • 开发者为执行每月免费额度,会记录每次请求的最少信息(时间、使用的服务、 token 数、成功与否),不含图片,也不含任何健康数值。

整个 app 不使用 AI 识别也能正常运行。AI 是按需选用,UI 上有明显标记。 识别服务连接不上时,您可以手动录入相同信息。

4. Apple 健康(HealthKit)集成 — 只读

您授权后,本应用会读取 Apple 健康中的体征数据和用药记录,自动填充到 您的档案。本应用不会写回 Apple 健康任何内容。您可随时在 iOS 设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备 → VitalScope 撤销授权。

5. 通知

本应用安排本地通知做服药提醒。所有提醒在设备本机调度,开发者看不到 您的提醒内容或时间。

6. 追踪与广告

本应用不跨 app / 网站追踪您。不接入第三方分析 SDK、广告 SDK,或 跨 app 标识符。隐私清单中 NSPrivacyTracking: false。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用面向成年用户。我们不主动收集 13 岁以下儿童数据。若家长用本应用 追踪儿童用药,本节第 2 条「本地 + 私有 iCloud」模式同样适用 —— 不会 进入开发者服务器(除非家长主动触发 AI 识别)。

8. 您的权利

您可以:

  • 删除账号:设置 → 账号 → 删除账号。会撤销 Sign in with Apple 授权,删除 开发者服务器上的账号和用量记录,并清除本机与您 iCloud 中的全部应用数据。 您手动导出的备份文件不受影响。
  • 删除任何记录或家庭成员(本地 + iCloud 同步删除)
  • 导出所有数据为备份文件(设置 → 备份与恢复)
  • 关闭 iCloud 同步(iOS 设置 → [Apple ID] → iCloud → VitalScope)
  • 不使用 AI 识别功能
  • 撤销 HealthKit 授权(iOS 设置 → 健康)
  • 删除应用(本地数据全部清除)

9. 变更

如有重大变更,我们会更新本页顶部日期,并在 App Store 下一次更新的「新功能」 里告知您。
