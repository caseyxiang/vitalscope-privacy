Frequently asked questions

  • Where is my data stored? On your device and in your own private iCloud. The developer cannot see it.
  • How do I back up or move to a new phone? Settings → 备份与恢复 (Backup & Restore) → export a backup file. iCloud sync also restores data automatically when you sign in with the same Apple ID.
  • AI scan says it can't reach the service. Your photos are kept — tap 手动录入 (Enter manually) to continue, or retry later on a different network (Wi-Fi / cellular). You can also save the report now and use 用照片智能识别 (Scan these photos) later.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → 账号 (Account) → 删除账号 (Delete Account). This erases your account, server-side usage logs and all app data on your devices and iCloud.
  • Is VitalScope medical advice? No. VitalScope is a record-keeping and reminder tool. Always consult your doctor.

常见问题

  • 我的数据存在哪里? 在您的设备和您本人的 iCloud 私有库里,开发者无法查看。
  • 怎么备份 / 换手机? 设置 → 备份与恢复 → 导出备份文件。用同一个 Apple ID 登录新手机时,iCloud 也会自动同步回来。
  • AI 识别提示连不上服务? 照片已保留,点「手动录入」继续填写,或换个网络(Wi-Fi / 蜂窝)后重试。也可以先保存报告,之后再点「用照片智能识别」补识别。
  • 怎么删除账号? 设置 → 账号 → 删除账号。会删除账号、服务器上的用量记录,以及您设备和 iCloud 中的全部应用数据。
  • VitalScope 能提供医疗建议吗? 不能。VitalScope 是记录和提醒工具,医疗决定请咨询医生。
